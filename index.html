Common questions

Which games are free?

Hive Mind, Maze Daze and Wormhole are free to play. One purchase, The Full Arcade, unlocks all ten games forever. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription and no ads.

I bought The Full Arcade. How do I get it on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open any locked game and tap Restore Purchases. It's also in Options. Your unlock usually appears on its own within a few seconds.

The visuals are too intense for me.

Open Options and lower Trip Intensity, set Flashing & Pulsing to Off or Low, slow the Visual Speed, or turn on Reduced Motion. Choosing Gentle Trip on the first screen sets all of these for you. If you or anyone watching has epilepsy or is sensitive to light, please talk to a doctor before playing.

Can it react to my own music?

Yes. In Options › Music, choose an audio file from your device, or let it listen through the microphone to music playing around you. Sound is analyzed on your device only. It is never recorded or uploaded.

How do the leaderboards work?

Each game has a Game Center leaderboard. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app and your best scores are posted automatically at the end of each game. Tap World Scores on the high-score screen to see them.

There's no sound.

Check the volume buttons, then Options › Music Volume and Sound FX Volume. Sound starts after your first tap.
