Privacy Policy

Psychedelic Arcade · Effective October 2, 2026

In short: Psychedelic Arcade does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Psychedelic Arcade is made by Agen-Tech ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles information. Questions go to groovalizer8@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app doesn't send data to us or to anyone else. It has no servers of its own, no analytics or advertising tools, and no third-party tracking code.

Information stored on your device

Your settings (such as visual intensity and volume) and your high-score tables are saved on your device only, so they're there next time you play. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone

If you choose the microphone as the music source, the app asks for permission and then measures the sound around you to drive the visuals. The audio is analyzed on your device in real time. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. You can turn microphone access off at any time in the Settings app.

Your music files

If you pick an audio file to play, it is read on your device only while the app runs. It is never copied or uploaded.

In-app purchases

The one-time Full Arcade purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase is active, so it can unlock the games.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
